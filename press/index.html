Short description

The galaxy is dying. The lanes are collapsing. Your squad is a random bunch of beings with nothing in common but a strong preference for not being unalived. Lumentide is a tactical roguelike about finding the route least likely to get you killed, and a story that makes perfect sense. Eventually.

About

Lumentide is a tactical turn-based roguelike about pretending you have a plan, finding the route least likely to get everyone killed, and dragging your crew through one more system before the darkening catches up.

  • Navigate a shifting, procedurally generated star map where every node is a fight, a gamble, or a tense breather.
  • Pre-load ships with weapons, shields, and specials; each turn, sockets fill with quantum-entangled options. Match two to five of a kind for escalating damage and shields.
  • Lead a squad of awkward, opinionated pilots, each with their own bonuses, specials, and personality.
  • Spoof sci-fi tone in the spirit of Galaxy Quest and Spaceballs: competent enough to survive, ridiculous enough to laugh at.
